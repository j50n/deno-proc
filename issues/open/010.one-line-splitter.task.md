# One line splitter

`toChunkedLines` (`.lines`, `.chunkedLines`) and `splitText` (JSON lines, the
record format) are the same algorithm twice: a fatal streaming decoder, the
invalid-UTF-8 recovery, split on a separator, carry the tail. The 0.28.0 fix
for naming the line of invalid UTF-8 had to land in both. From 008.

Decided: `toChunkedLines` becomes `splitText` on `"\n"` plus dropping one CR
from the end of each line. `splitText` and its two UTF-8 helpers move from
`transforms/common.ts` and `helpers.ts` to their own internal module,
`src/split-text.ts`, so the core doesn't import from `transforms/`.

Must not change: every line and message `.lines` and `.chunkedLines` give,
`Invalid UTF-8 at line N` after the lines before it, a CR at the very end
dropped, no empty last line after a final newline, and their speed.

Check: the suite; a seeded property test comparing the two on random bytes in
random chunks, run against the old `toChunkedLines` before it goes; the
comprehensive benchmarks and a 2M-line timing against 0.28.1.
